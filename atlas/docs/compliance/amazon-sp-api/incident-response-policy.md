# ATLAS Incident Response Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines how CodeExpo Global / ATLAS identifies, manages, contains, investigates, and reports security incidents.

## 2. Roles
- Incident Owner: Coordinates the incident response process.
- Technical Lead: Investigates technical impact, containment, remediation, and recovery.
- Security/Compliance Contact: Handles regulatory, contractual, and Amazon-related notifications.

## 3. Amazon Information Incident Notification
Any confirmed or suspected security incident involving Amazon Information must be reported to Amazon at security@amazon.com within 24 hours of detection.

## 4. Incident Response Process
1. Detect and record the incident.
2. Assess severity and affected systems/data.
3. Contain the incident.
4. Preserve relevant logs and evidence.
5. Investigate root cause.
6. Remediate the vulnerability or cause.
7. Recover affected services safely.
8. Notify required parties, including Amazon when Amazon Information is involved.
9. Document lessons learned and follow-up actions.

## 5. Incident Records
Each incident record must include:
- Detection date and time
- Affected systems
- Data potentially affected
- Severity
- Containment actions
- Root cause
- Remediation actions
- Notification details
- Closure date

## 6. Review Frequency
This Incident Response Policy and associated procedures must be reviewed at least once every 6 months and updated when material infrastructure, security, or regulatory changes occur.

## 7. Approval
This policy is adopted as the incident response standard for the ATLAS platform.
